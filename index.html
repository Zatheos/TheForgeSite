The Forge

A home for tabletop roleplay at your event

Bring The Forge to your event

The Forge is a tried-and-tested, self-contained tabletop RPG zone you can bring to new or existing conventions, gaming events and community gatherings.

Developed and operated by the York Board Games Convention, The Forge brings a curated network of Games Masters, a programme of tabletop roleplaying games, and all the organisation needed to make it happen.

We find, coordinate and pay the Game Masters and run The Forge during your event, so you can focus on setting the vibe, knowing your RPG zone is in safe hands. Whether you want to introduce RPGs to your convention for the first time or expand an existing programme, we'll work with you to find a flexible way to make it happen.

Every partnership is unique, so we scale to fit your event.

What The Forge provides

  1. A programme built for your crowd

    We work with you to plan a programme of roleplaying games that suits your attendees. We run GM submissions and keep the line-up balanced. (Yes, there is such a thing as too much D&D.)

  2. Game Masters, recruited and paid

    We recruit, coordinate and pay a curated network of GMs, so your volunteers and organisers don't have to.

  3. Scheduling and bookings

    We schedule every GM and session, and handle player bookings and payments for your attendees.

  4. Inducted, prepared GMs

    Every GM receives our induction covering safeguarding, accessibility, inclusion and player experience.

  5. On-site management

    We manage The Forge for the whole event. At least one team member on site is DBS checked and a trained Mental Health First Aider.

  6. Staging, comforts and cover

    We bring the staging and player comforts that make The Forge feel like The Forge, plus our own public liability insurance alongside yours.

What you provide

As a general starting point, we ask your event to provide:

  • Space in your venue for a welcome desk and for games to take place (agreed per event)
  • Furniture: tables (6ft × 3ft or 6ft × 2.5ft) and chairs
  • Announcements on your social media, website and mailing list when GM applications and RPG bookings open

What it costs you

No fee.

Apart from venue space and furniture, there is nothing to pay. We cover our costs through RPG ticket sales.
